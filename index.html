FAQ

Which STAR-CCM+ versions are supported?

Tested on Simcenter STAR-CCM+ 20.06 on Windows 11; Linux drivers are provided for PBS clusters. Recent earlier releases usually work; if a tool does not work on your setup, you get a refund.

Do I get the source code?

No. Tools are delivered as compiled, licensed builds. You configure them through documented settings files and command-line options.

Can my company buy several seats?

Yes. Each seat is one licence key. Email us for team pricing and invoices.

Do the tools send my simulation data anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your machine or cluster. The only network call is a short licence check that sends your key and an anonymous machine ID.

What if it does not work for me?

Email us within 14 days for a full refund. See the refund policy.
